Research · CSSTC 2026 · first author

A GaN HEMT compact model that holds at 500 °C

Commercial GaN compact models are calibrated to about 185 °C. Wildfire sensing, downhole drilling and planetary probes need transistors working at 200 to 500 °C. This work extends the industry-standard MVSG model into that range and validates it against measured devices from 22 °C to 500 °C.

Verilog-A · MVSG · Keysight IC-CAP · Wafer probing
