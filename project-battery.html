20 W Portable USB-C PD Battery Pack

5000 mAh single-cell Li-ion pack delivering 20 W over USB-C PD. Buck/boost output holds the PD voltage across the full state of charge, with over-current, under-current and over-temperature protection.

Architecture

Charge path and delivery path share one cell. A USB-PD front end feeds the charger, a fuel gauge and BMS supervise the cell, and an adjustable buck/boost stage feeds the output PD controller so the output voltage holds regardless of state of charge. TVS on the input, MCU coordinates charge, gauge and conversion.
